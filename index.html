Overview

R2DP: racoon2 external dataplane control protocol.

External dataplane control protocol for racoon2: drives a DPDK forwarder instead of the kernel via a PF_KEY-shaped protocol on a unix socket. Contributors own their work; contributions license its use to generate derivative output.
